Pilot Project

DigitAfrica — Our First Initiative

The inaugural CCK Hub project, demonstrating what university teams can achieve with CCK's infrastructure.

DigitAfrica is a pioneering collaborative platform designed to accelerate digital transformation across African universities. It brings together researchers, students, educators, and international partners under a shared technological ecosystem powered by CCK's Cloud and HPC capabilities.

This pilot serves as a proof of concept and a reference model for all future CCK Hub projects — demonstrating that world-class digital services can be built from within Tunisian academia.

  • Create a collaborative platform for university research and innovation
  • Foster cooperation between universities, researchers, and international partners
  • Develop digital tools for scientific research, teaching, and training
  • Leverage CCK HPC for advanced data processing and computation
  • Organise workshops, training sessions, and scientific events

Foundation

The Centre de Calcul El Khawarizmi is founded as Tunisia's national academic computing centre, named after the pioneering 9th-century mathematician Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi.

CCK deploys its first High Performance Computing clusters, providing Tunisian universities with local access to serious computational power for the first time.

CCK expands with a full cloud computing platform, enabling institutions to host services, run distributed workloads, and collaborate in real time.

Today

CCK Innovation Hub Opens

CCK launches the Innovation Hub — an open programme connecting university teams with CCK's full technology stack to build services that transform academic life across Tunisia.

Vision 2030

Regional Leadership

CCK aims to become the reference academic computing centre for the MENA region, hosting 50+ projects annually and serving 20+ partner universities.

Eligibility

Any team affiliated with a Tunisian university or academic institution can apply. This includes:

  • Students (undergraduate, master's, PhD)
  • Faculty members and researchers
  • Administrative staff with innovation projects
  • Multi-institution collaborative teams
  • Teams with international academic partners

Having confirmed funding strengthens your application, but it is not strictly required for an initial expression of interest. Before a formal agreement is signed, CCK will need confirmation that your project has or is actively seeking funding. CCK does not provide direct financial funding — it provides infrastructure and expert support.

International partners are welcome to collaborate within a project, but the lead team and principal investigator must be affiliated with a Tunisian university. Projects that foster international academic collaboration are viewed positively during review.

Submission

A complete proposal should include:

  • Full project description and objectives
  • Expected impact on the university community
  • Description of services to be built
  • Cloud and HPC resource requirements
  • Team composition with roles
  • Partners, sponsors, and funding sources
  • Project timeline with deliverables
  • Long-term sustainability plan

The CCK Hub operates rolling open calls — there is no fixed deadline. Proposals are reviewed continuously in the order they are received. CCK may publish specific themed calls with fixed deadlines. Follow CCK announcements to stay informed.

Yes, a team or individual can submit multiple project proposals. Each will be reviewed independently on its own merits. If multiple projects are accepted, the team will need to demonstrate sufficient capacity to deliver on all commitments.

Review Process

Initial review of an expression of interest typically takes 2–4 weeks. If invited to submit a full proposal, the formal review by the CCK expert panel takes a further 4–6 weeks. You will receive regular status updates throughout the process.

The expert panel evaluates proposals across five dimensions:

  • Technical feasibility — can it be built with CCK's infrastructure?
  • Scientific and academic merit
  • Community impact — how many people benefit, and how significantly?
  • Team capability and experience
  • Sustainability and long-term vision

All rejections include written feedback from the expert panel explaining the reasons and specific recommendations for improvement. Teams are encouraged to revise and resubmit. CCK's goal is to help good ideas reach maturity — not to act as a gatekeeper.

Resources & Support

Accepted projects receive access to:

  • Equipped collaborative workspace on CCK premises
  • Cloud infrastructure (VMs, storage, databases, Kubernetes)
  • HPC cluster allocation (CPU/GPU hours via SLURM)
  • Hardware, software licences, and development tools
  • Technical mentorship from CCK engineers and scientists
  • Cybersecurity auditing, administrative and legal guidance

For accepted projects, CCK provides infrastructure at no direct cost as part of the Hub programme. Resource allocations are defined in the project agreement based on need. Projects that scale significantly beyond agreed allocations may discuss adjusted resource plans with CCK.

Project Conditions

Standard conditions for all accepted projects:

  • Services must be accessible to the university community
  • Results may be featured by CCK in its communications
  • Compliance with CCK security and ethics standards
  • Documentation and knowledge sharing with the community
  • Participation in at least one annual CCK community event
  • Weekly or bi-weekly progress reporting

IP ownership is negotiated on a project-by-project basis and defined in the project agreement. In general, CCK respects the creative ownership of project teams. For projects with public funding, open-source outputs are strongly encouraged. CCK retains the right to feature the project in its communications and reports.

Community

There are many ways to engage with the CCK Hub community:

  • Attend CCK-organised workshops and seminars (open to all)
  • Volunteer as a mentor for active project teams
  • Contribute to open-source tools from Hub projects
  • Join as a collaborator within an existing team
  • Follow CCK announcements for themed challenges and hackathons

You can reach the CCK Hub team through the Contact page or by emailing hub@cck.tn. We aim to respond to all enquiries within 3 working days.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is here to help. Reach out directly and we'll be happy to assist.
